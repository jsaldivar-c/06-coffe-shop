# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Home coffee drinkers in Mexico who order beans or ground coffee online for a daily ritual (e.g. with milk in the morning, espresso, filter). Some subscribe for repeat delivery. Spanish-speaking (es-MX), prices in MXN.

## Product Purpose

Tulipán café is an online shop for specialty coffee, roasted in small batches and shipped fresh. Success: a visitor browses the range, picks a size and grind, and checks out (or subscribes) with confidence in freshness and origin.

## Positioning

Small-batch roasting (12 kg lots) with direct purchase from small producers, roasted weekly and shipped fresh. Story line: "Café de pocos, para muchos".

## Operating Context

Storefront flow: home page, shop listing with filters (Grano/Molido, roast Claro/Medio/Oscuro), product detail (size 250 g / 500 g / 1 kg, grind, subscription option with 10% discount), cart drawer. Free shipping from $500 MXN; delivery in 2-4 days nationwide. Payment methods listed: card, bank transfer, OXXO, Mercado Pago.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Stack: Astro with Tailwind CSS; products and site copy live in `src/config/`.
- Six products in the catalog (Huila, Chiapas, Yirgacheffe, Mezcla Casa, Oaxaca, Descafeinado), each with tasting notes, roast, origin data and a flavor profile.
- Nav links "Nosotros", "Suscripción" and "Contacto" are not built yet (placeholder `#`).
- Copy is in Spanish; keep terminology (grano, molido, tostado, lote).

## Brand Commitments

Name: Tulipán (suffix "café"). Spanish-language voice, warm and direct.

## Evidence on Hand

Everything is demo/placeholder content from a course project: reviews, sourcing claims, delivery promises, payments and the story photo are illustrative and unverified. Do not present them as confirmed facts or fabricate additional proof (customers, press, certifications, pricing beyond what exists).

## Product Principles

- Freshness and origin are the promise; show them concretely (roast date, origin, altitude, process).
- Approachable specialty: explain notes and roast plainly for newcomers.
- Make choosing easy: size, grind and subscription should be clear and fast.
- Be honest: never imply claims the business has not substantiated.
